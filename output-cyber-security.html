Cyber Security and Ethical Issues

On August 15, 2026, I actively participated in a one-hour professional development webinar entitled "Cyber Awareness: Digital Identity | Protect Yourself Online," organized by UpSkillTechPH Training Services. The session was led by Resource Speaker Vherbik Troy Cardenas, who provided practical insights on safeguarding digital footprints, securing online personal information, and recognizing modern cybersecurity threats.

Managing digital identity isn't a one-time setup — it's an ongoing practice.

Throughout the event, participants explored effective techniques for managing digital identity and maintaining privacy across modern online platforms. Upon completion of the training, Karl Laurence C. Piano was awarded an official Certificate of Participation (Credential ID: 75c0d67c661195951197353b3a542586), verified by Founder and Program Lead Alexander Lopez, highlighting a strong commitment to continuous learning and proactive digital safety practices.

01

Protecting your digital identity comes down to everyday habits — knowing what you share, spotting threats early, and staying deliberate about your online footprint.

02

03

Completing the training and earning a verified certificate reflects a real commitment to continuous learning and proactive digital safety.
