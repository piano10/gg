Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence

On August 18, 2026, I attended the online webinar "Multimedia Automation Using AI," a 1-hour session held via Google Meet and organized by Ethel Programming Computer Programming Services. The topic highlighted how artificial intelligence can be utilized in multimedia in ways beyond our imagination, making everyday tasks much easier.

AI is merely a tool designed to help us reach our goals — we shouldn't become overly dependent on it.

However, this does not mean AI-generated outputs are flawless; they still require human refinement, creativity, and knowledge. We should not become overly dependent on AI, as it is merely a tool designed to help us reach our goals. Like any technology, it comes with pros and cons. While it saves time, repurposes content, and improves accessibility, it can also produce inaccurate information, raise copyright concerns, and mislead media channels. Ultimately, we must practice responsibility in how we use AI.

Key Takeaways

01

AI can automate multimedia work in ways that save time, repurpose content, and improve accessibility.

02

AI-generated outputs are not flawless — they still need human refinement, creativity, and knowledge behind them.

03

Using AI responsibly means weighing its time-saving benefits against real risks like inaccuracy and copyright concerns.
